1. Who we are

Niftysphere (“we”, “us”) provides an online learning mobile application (“App”). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect and how we use it when you use the App.

Contact: mz.mzeeshan.dev@gmail.com (update this address if different).

2. Data we collect

Depending on how you sign up and use the App, we may collect:

  • Account details: full name, phone number, email address, password (stored hashed on our servers)
  • Identity / profile details: CNIC (or similar national ID), date of birth, gender, address / city / country
  • Authentication data: if you use Google Sign-In, we receive identifiers and profile information from Google / Firebase Authentication as permitted by that service
  • Learning activity: course enrollments, watch progress, saved / liked courses
  • Purchase records: invoices, coupon use, amounts and status (kept for bookkeeping even if you delete your account)
  • Technical data: device / app information needed to run and secure the App, and standard server logs

We do not currently use third-party advertising SDKs in the App.

3. Why we use your data

  • Create and manage your account
  • Verify your phone number (OTP)
  • Provide courses, enrollment, and progress
  • Process free / coupon enrollments and keep invoice records
  • Provide support and improve the App
  • Meet legal, accounting, and security obligations

4. Age

The App is not directed at children under 13. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account.

5. Sharing

We may share data with:

  • Service providers that help us run the App (for example hosting, databases, Firebase / Google Sign-In, SMS delivery for OTP)
  • Authorities when required by law

We do not sell your personal data.

6. Retention

We keep account and learning data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your user profile and related learning data such as enrollments, progress, and saved lists. We may retain invoices and related payment / coupon records for bookkeeping and legal purposes.

7. Your choices

  • Update profile details in the App where available
  • Delete your account in the App (Profile → Delete Account)
  • Contact us to ask about access or correction of your data

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International processing

Our servers and providers may process data in countries other than where you live. By using the App you understand that your data may be transferred as needed to provide the service.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will post the updated version on this page and change the “Last updated” date.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: mz.mzeeshan.dev@gmail.com
