Delete your Niftysphere account

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Niftysphere app account and what happens to your data.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Niftysphere Android app
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm when asked

Your account is deleted when that process finishes. You will be signed out.

If you cannot use the app

Email us from the address linked to your account (or include your phone number / account details so we can find you):

mz.mzeeshan.dev@gmail.com

Put Account deletion request in the subject line. We will process the request and confirm by email.

Data that is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

  • Your user profile (name, phone, email, password, CNIC, date of birth, gender, address, and similar account fields)
  • Course enrollments and lesson watch progress
  • Saved courses (My List) and likes
  • OTP / verification records tied to your phone
  • Firebase Authentication data for your account where applicable

Data that may be kept

We may keep invoices and related purchase / coupon records for bookkeeping, accounting, and requirements of financial institutions or applicable law. Those records may still show that a purchase or enrollment occurred, even after your account profile is gone.

We keep such records only as long as needed for those purposes, then remove or anonymize them where appropriate.
